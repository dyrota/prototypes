Async Deckbuilder Prototypes

Three small deckbuilders built for turn-by-turn asynchronous play. Every turn is self-contained, and none of them has a shared market row. Each one has a bot to test against.

Fair Folk

Your opponent splits every hand you draw into two piles. You choose which half to play, then you split their next hand.

Play

Loom

Every card links one symbol to another. You continue the shared thread from wherever your opponent left it, and leave them a symbol of your choosing.

Play

Relay

You both face the same twelve foes in the same order. Take one card from your offer; the two you pass up become your rival’s next offer.

Play

Games here are played against the bot or hotseat on one device, and saved in your browser. Async online play between people runs in each game’s Claude artifact version.
